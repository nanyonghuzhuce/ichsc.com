Training Programme

ICHSC high-pressure water jetting safety training

A practical training framework for personnel competence, jet control and worksite protection.

Training · ICHSC

The programme focuses on the controls that make high-pressure water jetting work auditable: equipment readiness, exclusion zones, protective equipment, communication and stop-work authority.

Training materials connect the work plan with field execution and close-out records, helping owners and contractors establish a consistent safety language.
